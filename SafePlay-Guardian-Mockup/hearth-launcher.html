Prototype overview

A calm dashboard for the family's screen time.

Hearth runs on the family PC. A parent opens this dashboard to see how their kids' devices are doing, set gentle limits, and stay honest about what each device can and can't enforce. Pick a screen to explore.

01 · Overview

Family home

At-a-glance status for each child. Devices waiting to be claimed are kept clearly separate.

 02 · Person

Devices

 05 · Policy

Schedule (weekly grid)

Paint the week: school, bedtime, free time. Drag across cells, save presets.

 06 · Policy

Monitoring

The single most important privacy choice. Enforcement-only is the visible default; detailed activity is an explicit opt-in.

 07 · Activity

Reports

Screen time over time, recent blocks, and a visible retention note. No creepy full browsing log.

 08 · Empty state

First open

No children, no devices, no nagging. A gentle three-step welcome for the first five minutes.

Principles applied across every screen

  • Calm over dense. Lots of whitespace, restrained color, no red-alert dashboards.
  • Honest about limits. When a protection can't be enforced on a device, the UI says so.
  • Privacy is visible. Monitoring level is a first-class control, not buried in settings.
  • Person-first. The family is people; devices belong to them, not the other way around.
