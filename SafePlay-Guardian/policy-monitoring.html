Maya /Policy/ Monitoring

How much do we monitor Maya?

Hearth never sends anything off this computer. But you still choose how much it watches and remembers. Enforcement only is what we recommend — it's enough to keep limits working without recording what Maya actually browses. The detailed level is a deliberate opt-in, and Maya should know if you turn it on.

Enforcement only

Recommended

Hearth keeps Maya inside her policy and records what was needed to do that. We don't keep a log of what she browsed.

  • Records when a block happens (e.g. "blocked roblox.com at 2:14 PM")
  • Counts total screen time so the budget works
  • Notes which device was in use
  • Does not record sites she visited that weren't blocked
  • Does not log app activity or screenshots

Detailed activity

Explicit opt-in

Hearth keeps a log of every site visited and app used. Useful only if you're actively working through a specific concern together. Strongly consider telling Maya first.

  • Everything above, plus:
  • Domains of every site visited (not full URLs)
  • Which apps were opened, and for how long
  • Never records keystrokes, messages, or screenshots — by design, not by setting

Activity older than 2 weeks is automatically deleted.

Nothing leaves this computer. We don't sync to the cloud, and we don't share with any other Hearth account. You can shorten the window or delete everything right now.
